Translate buttons in every language, and a much easier setup.

## Fixed

- **Works in any language.** If WhatsApp, WhatsApp Web or your Mac is set to German, Arabic, French or any language other than English, the Translate buttons were missing. They now appear in every language.
- **12-hour clocks.** On Macs showing times like "6:27 PM", WhatsApp Desktop sometimes couldn't find a voice note's audio. Fixed.
- **API key field.** The box to paste your key in Settings → Translation was invisible. It now has a border and a hint.

## Easier setup

- **Moves itself into Applications.** Opened straight from the disk image or your Downloads folder, the app now offers to move itself into Applications and reopens from there. Running from anywhere else is what made browsers report the app as "not installed" and made permissions hard to find.
- **Permission guide.** Settings → Connections → **Allow…** opens the right page in System Settings with a small guide beside it. Switch VoiceNote Translator on, or drag its icon from the guide straight into the list. No more **+** button and searching for the app. The guide closes and Settings turns green by itself.
- Settings → Connections shows Accessibility and Full Disk Access as two numbered steps, and the menu bar tells you when one is missing.

## Download

**`VoiceNote-Translator-1.1.1.dmg`**: open it and drag the app into Applications. Already on 1.1.0? Choose **Check for Updates…** in the menu bar, or wait for the automatic update.

Requires macOS 14 or later and an API key from [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) or any OpenAI-compatible provider.
