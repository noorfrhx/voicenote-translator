## Product Hunt copy

**Name:** VoiceNote Translator

**Tagline** (60 characters max): Translate WhatsApp voice messages on your Mac in one click

**Topics:** Mac, Productivity, Open Source, Artificial Intelligence

**Description** (260 characters max):
> A Translate button next to every voice message in WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp for Mac. One click and you read what was said, in plain English or any language. Your API key stays in your Keychain. Free and open source.

**First comment (maker):**
> Hi Product Hunt! I get a lot of voice messages in languages I don't speak, and replaying them never helped. So I built VoiceNote Translator.
>
> It puts a Translate button next to every voice message, in WhatsApp Web (through a small browser extension) and in the WhatsApp desktop app (through a read-only overlay). Click it, and a few seconds later you read what they said, in plain English, with the original transcript one click away.
>
> A few things I cared about:
> • **Private:** your API key stays in your Mac's Keychain, audio goes only to the model you choose (OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible API), and the extension has no network access at all.
> • **Cheap:** a one-minute voice note costs well under a cent, and each note is only ever translated once.
> • **Native:** a real menu bar app that opens at login and updates itself, with about 1–2% CPU while WhatsApp is in front.
>
> It's free and MIT-licensed: github.com/noorfrhx/voicenote-translator. I'd love to hear which languages you try it with, and what you'd want next.

**Links:** Website / download: `https://github.com/noorfrhx/voicenote-translator` · Video: your YouTube link of the 16:9 film.
